Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Shorts Cutter ("the Application") is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. The authors do not operate any server for it and do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data.

Data from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the Application receives:

  • your TikTok open_id, display name and avatar URL (user.info.basic) - used only to show which account is connected;
  • access and refresh tokens - used only to upload the videos you choose to your TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload).

This data is stored only in a local folder on your computer. It is sent nowhere except back to TikTok's own API when you upload a video or refresh access.

Videos

Your videos are processed locally. A video is sent to TikTok (or YouTube) only when you explicitly choose to upload it.

Deleting your data

Click "Disconnect" in the Application to delete the stored tokens and account details for an account. You can also revoke the Application's access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13.
