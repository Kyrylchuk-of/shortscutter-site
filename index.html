Shorts Cutter

Shorts Cutter is a desktop application for creators. It helps you cut your own long videos into short vertical clips (9:16), add subtitles, a caption and a logo, and export them in high quality.

TikTok integration

After you connect your TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit, Shorts Cutter can upload an exported clip to your TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, video.upload). You then open the notification in the TikTok app, add a description, hashtags and music, and publish it yourself. Shorts Cutter never publishes on your behalf.

How it works

  • The application runs locally on your computer; there is no Shorts Cutter server or user account.
  • Requested permissions: user.info.basic (to show the connected account's name and avatar) and video.upload (to send drafts).
  • You can disconnect an account in the application at any time, or revoke access in your TikTok settings.
